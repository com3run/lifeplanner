# 01 — UK Launch Campaign (Meta Ads)

**Last updated**: 2026-04-23
**Campaign window**: Q2 2026 (launch Monday of Week 2, approximately May 4)
**Total budget**: $4,500 over 12 weeks ($1,500/mo)
**Primary geography**: United Kingdom
**Primary objective**: App Promotion → App Installs (iOS + Android) with in-app subscription conversion beginning Week 9.

---

## 1. Why UK first — one-paragraph justification

UK gives us the best combination of (a) paid-conversion quality (premium wellness apps see 6–12% free-to-paid), (b) English-only creative (zero translation), and (c) manageable CPI for our budget (£3–£5 for lifestyle apps vs. £7–£10 in the US). Every learning — winning hook, creative fatigue curves, paywall conversion — transfers directly to Canada/Australia/US. One country, hard, is better signal than four countries, thin.

## 2. Campaign architecture

### Campaign level
- **Objective**: App Promotion.
- **Performance goal**: App installs (Week 1–8), then **maximize number of purchase events** once Phase 12 monetization is live (Week 9+).
- **Budget type**: **Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO) OFF** initially, **ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization) ON** — gives us visibility on which country/audience wins. Switch to CBO in Week 5 once we know who's winning.
- **Attribution setting**: 7-day click / 1-day view. Use Meta's default.

### Ad sets (Week 1 launch)

Three ad sets. One country (UK). One language (English). This is tight on purpose.

| Ad set | Daily budget | Audience | Placement | Optimization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AS-A** — Broad | £12 | UK, age 25–40, all genders, no interest targeting | Automatic | App installs |
| **AS-B** — Interest | £12 | UK, age 25–40, interests: `Self-improvement`, `Personal development`, `Mental health awareness`, `Mindfulness`, `Life coaching` | Automatic | App installs |
| **AS-C** — Advantage+ App | £12 | Meta chooses everything | Meta-controlled | App installs |

**Weekly spend**: ~£250 across the three sets (≈ $315). Monthly budget ~£1,000 ≈ $1,260 — leaves buffer for boosting winners.

### Creative quantity per ad set
Each ad set gets **3 creatives** rotating (see §3). **Do not exceed 3**; Meta's v6 algorithm needs 10–20 conversions per creative per week to learn, and below that threshold creatives starve each other.

### Learning phase rule
Do **not** touch ad sets for the first 72 hours after launch. Do not rename, do not adjust budget, do not kill underperformers. Meta counts any edit as a reset to learning phase. Look at data after 72 hours.

## 4. Landing page — `lifeplanner.tribe.az/download`

Currently: a redirect to App Store / Play Store.

**For Week 1, do NOT redesign.** Keep the direct redirect. Running ads straight-to-store is faster and attributes cleanly.

**For Week 5 (start of month 2)**, upgrade to a match quiz flow at `lifeplanner.tribe.az/match`:

### Match quiz requirements

- 8 questions max, each single-select, takes 60 seconds.
- Maps answers to the 7 life categories via weighted scoring.
- Result page: "Your top coaches this week: [Coach 1 name + portrait + one-line]" with secondary coach underneath.
- Below the result: two big buttons — **App Store** and **Play Store**.
- Optional email capture ("Send me my match and weekly insights" — pre-checked; not required to proceed).
- Meta Pixel events fired: `QuizStart`, `QuizComplete`, `StoreClick`, `EmailCapture`.
- Mobile-first; must load < 1.5s on 4G. Tailwind or plain CSS, no heavy frameworks.

### 8 questions (first draft)

1. **What's heaviest on your mind right now?** (a) Career (b) Money (c) Body / health (d) Relationships (e) Emotions (f) Purpose / meaning (g) Family
2. **Your ideal coach would be…** (a) Strategic and ambitious (b) Warm and patient (c) Sharp and direct (d) Calm and reflective
3. **Most of your stress comes from…** (a) Not enough time (b) Not enough money (c) Not enough energy (d) Not enough clarity
4. **What did you last try to improve in your life?** (a) Fitness (b) Work / career (c) Spending (d) Relationships (e) Headspace (f) Something spiritual
5. **How do you usually set goals?** (a) Lists (b) Calendars (c) Only when pushed (d) Rarely — I wing it
6. **You sleep…** (a) 4–5 hrs (b) 6 hrs (c) 7–8 hrs (d) Depends on the week
7. **Your friends would describe you as…** (a) Driven (b) Thoughtful (c) Fun (d) Reliable (e) Independent
8. **When you imagine your life in 5 years, the biggest unknown is…** (a) My career (b) My relationships (c) My money (d) My health (e) My purpose

Scoring: each answer adds weight to one or two of the 7 categories; result = top-2 coaches whose categories accumulate most weight.

## 5. Tracking & measurement setup

### Meta-side
- **Meta App Events SDK** firing `install`, `session_start`, `goal_created`, `coach_chat_started`, `habit_checkin`. These are the only 5 events we need in v1.
- **Conversion API** (CAPI) integrated server-side via Supabase Edge Function. This is mandatory — iOS 14.5+ kills pixel-only attribution.
- **Meta Pixel** on the landing page (already there, verify in Ads Manager > Events Manager).

### App analytics
- **Firebase Analytics** already in place. Configure custom audience "7-day retained" from the `session_start` event.
- **PostHog free tier** for funnel analysis (install → first chat → first goal → paywall view → purchase). Does not require schema design; fires from SDK events.

### Reporting cadence
- **Daily (30 seconds)**: glance at ads manager spend, CPI, CTR. No decisions.
- **Day 4 + Day 7 (15 minutes)**: first decision point. Which ad set leads in CPI × CTR? Which creative is fatigued (frequency > 2.5)?
- **End of Week 2 (1 hour)**: first real retro. See §7 template.

## 9. Risks + mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| UK CPI runs hotter than £5 consistently | Medium | Switch primary to Canada Week 3; creative transfers 1:1. |
| Learning phase never stabilizes (< 50 installs / ad set / week) | Medium | Consolidate to 2 ad sets by Week 3, not 3. |
| D1 retention < 30% | Medium-High | Fix onboarding before scaling ads. See `coach-collaboration-and-profile-aware-goals.md` §5 — the Luna bootstrap flow is the user's first 2 minutes. |
| App Store / Play Store reject in review | Low-Medium | Submit 2 weeks before Week 2 launch. Have fallback: launch with existing listing, iterate. |
| Monetization conversion < 3% in Week 9–10 | Medium | Paywall placement is the lever, not price. Try context-based soft paywalls before raising price. |
| Creative fatigue | High (inevitable) | Rotate fresh creative at Weeks 3, 5, 7, 9, 11. Budget for 2 creative refreshes per month. |
| Ad account restriction (Meta policy) | Low | Keep copy claims conservative. No "cures", no "guaranteed results", no "lose X kg". |
