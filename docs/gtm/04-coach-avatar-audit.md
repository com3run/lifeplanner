# 04 — Coach Avatar Audit

**Last updated**: 2026-04-23
**Scope**: Assessment of the 7 current coach avatars in `docs/coach-avatars/` against the master visual framework in `03-coach-visual-identity.md` and the UK-premium positioning in `00-strategy.md`.
**Status**: Decision required — the current avatars and the framework represent two different visual directions. This doc forces the choice.

---

## 1. What's actually there today

In `docs/coach-avatars/` you have 7 canonical portraits at **768×1376 (3:4 portrait)**, one per coach, saved as both high-res `.png` (~2 MB each) and low-res `.jpg` thumbnails (~2.5 KB). Same framing, same aspect ratio, same era of production (March 2026).

All 7 share one clear visual language:

- **Medium**: illustrated / stylized digital painting. Semi-realistic, softened, polished. Think "high-end mobile app mascot" closer to Duolingo / Finch / modern Headspace than Aitana López or Pi visuals.
- **Framing**: chest-and-shoulders portrait, slight off-centre, subject looks toward camera with a warm expression.
- **Background**: solid-colour gradient per coach with floating symbols (stars, leaves, coins, hearts, energy lines) that visually tag the life domain.
- **Colour-as-domain code**: each coach has a distinct colour world — purple / navy / green / red / orange / teal / gold. Strong wayfinding; users will learn "Luna = purple, Morgan = green" within a session.
- **Lighting / mood**: warm, friendly, approachable. Nothing in this set reads as "cold luxury" or "editorial fashion campaign".

**Consistency score across the 7: high.** This set was clearly art-directed together.

## 3. The real decision — three paths

### Path A — Keep the illustrated style; rewrite the framework

Keep the 7 avatars as your permanent visual system; treat illustrated semi-realism as the Tribe brand. Update `03-coach-visual-identity.md` to describe THIS style instead of the photorealistic framework.

- **Pros**: zero rework cost, consistency already there, fits "warm / friendly / belonging" better than cold luxury, differentiated from photoreal AI apps (Pi, Replika default) that look samey.
- **Cons**: risks being perceived as "casual mobile app" rather than "premium coaching product" at £6.99/mo price point. UK premium wellness buyers are trained to pay for products that *look* like Calm (photographic) or BetterUp (editorial).
- **Who this fits**: if Tribe's real audience is 25–40 wellness-curious women with "Finch-adjacent" taste.
- **Cost**: ~4 hours to rewrite the framework.

### Path B — Regenerate all 7 in photoreal cinematic style (keep framework)

Commission 7 new hero portraits matching `03-coach-visual-identity.md` exactly. Retire the illustrated avatars to `docs/coach-avatars/archive/`. Migrate the app UI to use the new photoreal images.

- **Pros**: premium positioning earned visually, cinematic ads become trivially strong, price point (£6.99–£49/year) feels like value-for-money rather than surcharge.
- **Cons**: 2–3 weeks identity-lock testing per coach × 7 coaches (parallelizable to ~4–6 weeks), in-app art style flip is a meaningful product change, risk of losing the warm friendliness the current set has in spades.
- **Who this fits**: if Tribe is competing for the Calm / Headspace / BetterUp user and positioning against Replika's anime-style.
- **Cost**: 4–6 weeks of creative production; pause or delay the UK launch accordingly.

### Path C — Hybrid: illustrated in-app, photoreal for marketing (**recommended**)

Keep the 7 illustrated avatars in the app (chat UI, onboarding, coach cards, Life Balance Wheel wedges). Commission **separate photoreal hero portraits** for marketing and store-listing assets only (ads, landing page hero, App Store screenshots, social).

- **Pros**: strongest of both worlds — the warm friendliness of illustrated in-product, the premium aspiration of photoreal in marketing. Matches what Calm, Headspace, and Fabulous all do (illustrated in-app, photographic in ads). No art-direction flip mid-session for users. Parallel pipelines, not sequential.
- **Cons**: two asset pipelines to maintain, slight risk of "which coach looks like what?" confusion if the illustrated and photoreal portraits don't recognizably share face. Mitigate: treat illustrated as the **canonical identity reference** for the photoreal generation (same hair, same wardrobe palette, same age intention).
- **Cost**: ~1–2 weeks to generate the 7 photoreal hero portraits (using the current illustrated avatars as the face-reference input to Midjourney's `--cref`, which preserves the identity signal cross-style).
- **This is what I recommend.**
